    actor U as Boutique (utilisateur)
    participant S as Système
    participant ST as Stripe

    ref over U,S: Chargement des plans + subscription access

    end

    opt Changer l'intervalle de facturation
        Note right of U: voir subscriptionsys/select_billing.txt

    end

        Note right of U: voir subscriptionsys/subscribe_stripe.txt
        ref over U,S,ST: Stripe Checkout (redirection + paiement + webhook)
    else Déjà abonné — Upgrade
        Note right of U: voir subscriptionsys/upgrade_plan.txt
        ref over U,S: Upgrade (proration credit)

        Note over U,S: Bouton "Current Plan" désactivé
    else Déjà abonné — Plan inférieur
        Note over U,S: Aucun bouton (downgrade interdit)
    end
